Immediate Intervention Plan

Triage Window: 48 Hours

Follow this clinical protocol to arrest secondary spread across adjoining blocks of your orchard.

1

Surgical Pruning & Sanitation

Prune diseased twigs at least 5cm below the necrotic margins. Disinfect shears with 1% Sodium Hypochlorite after each cut. Burn clippings off-site.

2

Micro-Canopy Drainage Correction

Reduce micro-sprinkler intervals. Excessive humidity above 82% accelerates flagellar bacterial invasion. Elevate root-zone trenches to eliminate stagnant runoff.

3

Targeted Bactericide Spray Schedule

Apply Copper Oxychloride (COC 50% WP) @ 2.5g/L combined with Streptocycline @ 0.5g/L. Spray during early morning (6:30 - 9:00 AM) prior to thermal wind.

Frequently Consulted

Frequently Asked Diagnostic Questions

Everything you need to know about precision foliar diagnostics, sunlight interference, and offline usage in remote areas.

wb_sunny

Does blinding sunlight alter the diagnosis?

No. The camera pipeline incorporates automated dynamic tone-mapping specifically trained against harsh 12:00 PM Indian sunlight, reducing spectral glare on oily or waxy leaves.

offline_bolt

What happens if there is zero mobile 4G/5G?

The Kisan AI model weighs only 2.4 MB and runs natively inside your browser or mobile progressive application. No cloud inference is required for disease detection or audio playbook playback.

local_pharmacy

Are agrochemical spray amounts certified safe?

Every spray schedule aligns with CIBRC (Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee) and Indian Council of Agricultural Research guidelines, strictly outlining protective intervals before harvesting.

record_voice_over

Can elderly farmers use voice guidance?

Yes. Audio guidance operates in regional Marathi and Hindi dialects, speaking chemical brand names, water measurements, and pruning instructions without requiring text literacy.
